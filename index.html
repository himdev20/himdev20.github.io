Hi, I’m Himanshu! Welcome to my website. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Economics at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur.

I am interested in understanding the economic forces that shape inequality, development, and public policy. My academic interests lie primarily in macroeconomics and development economics, particularly in questions surrounding poverty, food security, and the distributional consequences of technological change. I am especially curious about how the ongoing AI revolution may reshape labour markets, economic opportunities, and inequality.

At IIT Kanpur, I have worked on research examining the effects of Trade Policy Uncertainty and Geopolitical Risk on financial markets, using econometric methods and machine learning. I have also explored the economics of last-mile public-service delivery, studying how vehicle capacity and working-time constraints affect the cost of reaching underserved communities.

Beyond academics, I have explored the applications of quantitative methods through internships in quantitative research at Quadeye Securities and data science at American Express. These experiences have allowed me to work with optimization, financial data, and generative AI while developing a deeper interest in the intersection of economics, technology, and decision-making.

Outside academics, I enjoy being involved in campus activities and organizing book club events. I am always interested in conversations about economics, technology, public policy, and the ways in which research can help us better understand the world around us.
